# Industrial Machinery Sales & Growth Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This project is a Power BI business intelligence solution designed to analyze the sales and financial performance of a fictional industrial machinery supplier serving food-manufacturing businesses.

The goal was to understand how sales data can help a business identify its strongest revenue-generating products, evaluate customer and regional performance, monitor profitability, track sales targets, and discover potential growth opportunities.

The idea came from my own imagination of presenting a business performance report in a conference room to managers and senior employees. I wanted to explain not only what the numbers showed, but also how the company could use those insights to make better business decisions and plan its future strategy.

Rather than simply creating charts to demonstrate Power BI features, I wanted to approach the project from a business analyst's perspective by connecting data, business questions, insights, and potential actions.

## 2. Project Purpose

The primary purpose of this project was to explore how business intelligence can support sales growth and better decision-making.

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- Which products generate the most revenue, and what is the No. 1 revenue-generating product?
- Which are the top 10 products by recognized revenue?
- Which product categories contribute most to overall revenue?
- Which customers and customer segments contribute significantly to business performance?
- How are regions performing against their sales targets?
- What do revenue, gross profit, and gross margin reveal about financial performance?
- What potential revenue opportunities exist in the spare-parts pipeline?
- How can managers use these insights to identify opportunities and make more informed future business decisions?

## 3. Dashboard Features

The Power BI report contains four analytical pages.

### Page 1 — Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of overall business performance through key performance indicators, monthly recognized revenue trends, and regional sales performance.

**Purpose:** Help management quickly understand the company's overall performance and identify areas that may require attention.

### Page 2 — Customer & Segment Strategy

Analyzes customer performance, customer segments, industries, and revenue and gross profit contributions.

**Purpose:** Help identify valuable customer groups, understand where revenue comes from, and support more focused customer relationship and sales strategies.

### Page 3 — Product & Spare Parts Analysis

Examines product revenue, the top 10 products by revenue, product-category performance, and the weighted spare-parts opportunity pipeline.

**Purpose:** Identify important revenue-generating products and explore potential opportunities for additional business.

### Page 4 — Regional Growth & Financial Performance

Compares actual recognized revenue with sales targets, examines target achievement by region, and tracks operating expenses over time.

**Purpose:** Help management monitor performance against targets, investigate regional differences, and review financial trends.

## 4. Technical Work and Tools

The project was developed using Power BI and Excel, with ChatGPT assisting during parts of the development process.

Key technical components include:

- Preparing and organizing a multi-table synthetic business dataset.
- Creating relationships between relevant tables in the Power BI data model.
- Using DAX measures to calculate recognized revenue, estimated gross profit, gross margin, delivered orders, target achievement, operating expenses, and the weighted spare-parts pipeline.
- Building KPI cards, bar charts, trend charts, and analytical tables.
- Comparing product, customer, category, and regional performance.
- Organizing the report into four pages based on distinct business questions.

**Tools used:** Microsoft Power BI, DAX, Microsoft Excel, and ChatGPT-assisted development.

## 5. Key Findings and Business Insights

The current dashboard highlights several findings from the synthetic dataset:

- **Top revenue-generating product:** Tray Sealer Pro, with approximately $5.4 million in recognized revenue.
- **Second-highest product:** Bottling Line Pro, with approximately $5.1 million in recognized revenue.
- **Leading product category:** Packaging Lines, with approximately $27 million in recognized revenue.
- **Second-leading product category:** Sealing Machines, with approximately $22 million in recognized revenue.
- **Weighted spare-parts pipeline:** Approximately $819.58K in potential pipeline value.
- **Delivered orders:** 1,118, as displayed on the Executive Overview dashboard.
- **Executive Overview gross margin:** Approximately 28.18%, as displayed on the dashboard.

These figures provide a starting point for investigating which products and business areas deserve further attention. Additional findings from the customer, regional, target, and financial analysis will be incorporated into the final report after validation.

## 6. How These Insights Could Support Future Business Strategy

The dashboard demonstrates how sales and financial information can be translated into practical business questions and potential actions.

### Product strategy

Management could investigate why Tray Sealer Pro and Bottling Line Pro lead in revenue, including customer demand, pricing, order frequency, and profitability. These findings could inform product-specific sales plans and inventory discussions.

### Customer strategy

Customer and industry analysis could help identify valuable customer groups, understand purchasing patterns, and guide targeted outreach, repeat-business efforts, and relationship development.

### Regional sales strategy

Comparing actual revenue with sales targets could help management identify regions performing above or below expectations and investigate the reasons behind those differences.

### Profitability and financial monitoring

Reviewing gross profit, gross margin, and operating expenses together could help management investigate whether strong revenue is translating into attractive profitability and where costs deserve closer examination.

### Spare-parts opportunities

The weighted spare-parts pipeline could help sales teams prioritize follow-ups and evaluate potential additional revenue opportunities, while distinguishing pipeline value from revenue already recognized.

## 7. Potential Business Impact

If applied to accurate real-world business data and supported by appropriate management action, this type of dashboard could contribute to:

- More informed and timely business decisions.
- Better visibility into product and customer performance.
- More focused sales and customer-retention efforts.
- Improved monitoring of regional sales targets.
- Earlier identification of financial trends that require investigation.
- More systematic follow-up on potential spare-parts opportunities.

These are potential benefits, not measured outcomes from this portfolio project. The dashboard supports analysis and decision-making; actual business impact would need to be evaluated using real company data and subsequent performance results.

## 9. Dataset Disclaimer

This project uses a **synthetic dataset created for learning and portfolio purposes**. It represents a fictional industrial machinery supplier serving food-manufacturing businesses.

The dataset includes sales transactions, customers, products, monthly sales targets, operating expenses, and spare-parts opportunities. Although the data is synthetic, the business questions and analytical methods are inspired by realistic business intelligence challenges.

All financial figures and findings are illustrative. They do not represent the actual performance of a real company, and no real increase in revenue, profit, or cost savings is claimed.
